Privacy Policy

King (journey & streak tracker) · Effective September 5, 2026

Short version: Your journey data stays on your device. We do not run a King account or King cloud database. Optional premium purchases are handled by Google Play. You can export, import, or delete your data in the app.

1. Who we are

This policy describes how the mobile application King (“the app”, package name com.kingtracker.app) handles information. King is a personal habit and journey tracking tool.

2. Information the app stores

When you use King, the app stores data locally on your device (for example in on-device storage). This may include:

  • Daily logs (strong days and slips)
  • Journey scores, streaks, and milestone progress
  • Calendar and history used inside the app
  • App settings, including reminder time if you enable reminders
  • Local trial and premium access state (cached on device after Google Play confirms a purchase)
  • Timestamp of your last backup export, if you use backup

We do not require you to create a King account. We do not upload your journey logs to our own servers.

3. Information we do not collect

As of this policy date, King does not:

  • Collect your name, email, or phone number inside the app
  • Use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the app
  • Sell your personal information
  • Sync your journey data to a King-operated cloud service

4. Backup and restore

You may export your progress to a file on your device (or a location you choose through your phone’s share/save flow). Import reads a file you select. Those files are under your control. We do not receive them unless you send them to someone yourself.

5. Premium purchases (Google Play)

If you subscribe to King Premium, payment and subscription management are handled by Google Play. Google processes payment information according to its own privacy policy:

Google Privacy Policy

The app may store a local cache of premium access (for example trial start date or subscription access window) so features work offline. That cache is not a substitute for Google’s billing records.

6. Notifications and permissions

If you turn on daily reminders, the app may ask for:

  • Notifications — to show your reminder
  • Exact alarms (on some Android versions) — to schedule the time you choose

You can deny permissions; core logging still works without reminders. Reminder scheduling may be limited if permissions are denied.

7. Internet access

The app may use the internet to:

  • Load subscription prices and complete purchases through Google Play
  • Restore purchases through Google Play

Day-to-day journey logging does not require an internet connection.

8. Children

King is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help you delete local data using in-app reset or uninstall.

9. How to delete your data

  • In the app: use Backup & Reset → Reset All Data to clear progress on the device.
  • Uninstall the app to remove local app data from your device.
  • Backup files you saved elsewhere must be deleted by you in the folder or app where you stored them.

10. Data retention

Data remains on your device until you reset the app, import different data, or uninstall. We do not retain copies on King servers.

11. Security

Your data is stored in the app’s private storage on your device, protected by your device’s operating system. Export files are only as secure as where you save them. Keep backup files private if they contain sensitive information.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes (for example if we add cloud sync or analytics). We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
