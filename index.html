    📖 Learn the Journey

    Why this app?

    This app helps you build self-control through consistent daily progress. It focuses on long-term improvement, not perfection.

    Journey vs Streak

    A streak shows your current run.

    A Journey shows how much you've improved over time.

    One relapse may end a streak, but it doesn't erase your progress.

    How to use the app

    • Check in once a day.
    • Be honest with yourself.
    • Try to improve your Journey score over time.
    • Don't worry about being perfect.

    If you relapse

    Record it and move on.

    Don't quit because of one bad day. Every new day is another opportunity to improve.

    Your goal

    Don't ask:

    "Can I keep a perfect streak forever?"

    Ask:

    "Can I make this Journey better than the last one?"

    Your data

    Your data stays on your device. You can export and import it whenever you want.

    One final thought

    Progress is measured over months, not moments. Keep showing up. Small improvements become lasting change.
