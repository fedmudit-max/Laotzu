    How King Works

    Log Every Day

    When you tap I Stayed Strong Today, you're not just logging a day.

    You're registering proof.

    Every thought you fought.
    Every impulse you didn't react to.
    Every moment you chose differently.

    It counts. It's recorded. It's part of your history.

    When you tap I Slipped, you're being honest.

    You acknowledge what happened. You take responsibility.

    Logging a slip is an act of courage. It means you're serious enough about change to face the truth.

    Don't hide the number. Log it.

    The Journey System

    Don't just count your streak. Measure the Journey.

    Every Journey starts with 10 Powers.

    Think of them as something valuable — something you don't want to waste.

    Every time you slip, one Power is lost.

    10→9→8→7.......→3→2→1

    More powers you have, stronger you are. Less powers you have, weaker you are.

    Every time you relapse, you lose one Power — and you feel this:

    I have lost one. I don't want to lose another.

    Your goal isn't simply to protect a streak.

    Your goal is to achieve as many strong days as possible before the Journey ends.

    When you're climbing a mountain and you slip, you don't start again from the bottom.

    You might spend your whole life trying to reach the mountain top in one go, or through one perfect streak.

    You learn, improve, find your footing, and keep moving forward.

    An example of a Journey:

    Streak 1:4 days→slip→4 strong / 1 slip

    Streak 2:5 days→slip→9 strong / 2 slips

    Streak 3:2 days→slip→11 strong / 3 slips

    Streak 4:0 days→slip→11 strong / 4 slips

    Your strong days accumulate.

    Your Powers count down.

    Eventually, the Journey ends.

    25 strong days / 10 slips.

    And now you have a number. A number you can understand. A number you can beat.

    The Journey Score

    Journey Score is simple: Strong Days / Slips

    It tells you how much strength you've built relative to the number of slips during the Journey.

    Before King, you may have slipped countless times with nothing to show for it. No record of your strong days. No measure of how often you were failing.

    No system to understand whether you were improving.

    Here, you have a success-against-failure number and a clear goal:

    Make the next Journey better.

    25 / 10→30 / 10

    That's 5 more strong days. A 20% improvement.

    Now, 30 becomes your new score to beat in the next Journey.

    A low score like 12 / 10 shows where your Journey stands today. It gives a benchmark to improve. It shows that you're slipping more often than you'd like.

    A score in the hundreds, like 145 / 5 even with occasional slips, shows how far you've come.

    Imagine a Journey Score of 180 / 10. The number speaks for itself. It shows stamina. It shows control. It shows accumulated progress.

    Such a person is in a far better situation than someone who keeps resetting his streak to 0 and trying again and again.

    Focus on the Journey, Streak will follow.

    King Remembers

    Not just where you are today, but where you've been.

    • Your streaks — but most importantly, your Best Streak
    • Your milestone achievements
    • Your number of 50-day streaks
    • Your number of 100-day streaks
    • Your Journeys — but most importantly, your Best Journey

    Every time you reach a milestone — 50 days, 100 days, a personal best — King records it as part of your history.

    Imagine your record showing:

    50-day streaks: 3

    100-day streaks: 2

    Personal Best: 120 days

    Best Journey: 180 / 10

    Your current streak could be zero. But your history would say you've reached a 50-day streak three times and a 100-day streak twice. You've gone all the way to 120 in one streak. And you've built a Journey of 180 strong days.

    The Monthly Mirror

    It reflects your behaviour.

    Log honestly — the strong days and the slips.

    When you see more green, you see the days you chose strength. You see the proof that you're going strong.

    There is a powerful feeling in discovering that you are capable of being stronger than you thought.

    When you see more red, the numbers tell another truth. They show you where you're slipping more than you'd like.

    Imagine 23 strong days with 7 slips.

    Look at that honestly.

    Maybe you've improved from where you were before. You'll feel it. Maybe you've slipped more than before. That will hurt. Either way, you win — because you now know the truth.

    If you've improved, you have proof that you're getting stronger. If you've fallen, you have a clear signal that something needs to change.

    The Monthly Mirror doesn't punish you. It shows you where you are.

    What you see honestly, you can change.

    One Week at a Time

    The weekly timeline keeps your focus where it belongs.

    Not a year-long streak. Not a perfect record.

    Just two things:

    Be strong today. Be strong this week.

    The long-term goal of "not failing next time" is too vague to hold onto in a difficult moment.

    That's why King narrows the window.

    Day 1. Day 2. Day 3. Day 4 .... Day 6. Day 7.

    A progress dot moves toward the end of the current day every 8 hours, giving you a visible sign of progress.

    When the week ends, you start again from Day 1 — with the same humility, the same beginner's mind.

    Small windows. Clear targets. Real progress.

    Can You Change Yourself?

    In the beginning, you may struggle to reach even 50 days. Maybe even 25.

    Don't lose faith.

    One more strong day than your last Journey is a sign of progress.

    That's all it needs to be.

    The number in the hundreds will come.

    Don't rely only on motivation.

    Build evidence.

    Every strong day.
    Every green square.

    Every new personal best.

    I can change. I have control.

    One day. One week. One Journey at a time.
