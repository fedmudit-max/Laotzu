King Privacy Policy

King (Journey & Streak Tracker)
Effective date: September 5, 2026

Short version

Your King journey data is stored on your device. King does not require an account and does not operate a cloud service that stores your journey logs.

If you purchase King Premium, Google Play handles the payment and subscription process. King may store limited billing-related access information locally on your device so Premium features can work and be restored.

You can export, import, or delete your journey data using features provided in the app.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application King (“King”, “the app”, or “we”) handles information.

King is a personal journey and habit-tracking application. The Android package name is com.kingtracker.app.

2. Information stored by King

King stores information locally on your device so that the app can provide its journey-tracking features.

Depending on how you use the app, locally stored information may include:

  • Daily journey logs, including strong days and slips
  • Journey scores, streaks, milestones, and related progress
  • Calendar and history information displayed within the app
  • App settings, including your reminder preferences and reminder time
  • Local information used to determine trial or Premium access
  • The timestamp of your most recent backup export, if you use the backup feature

King does not require you to create a King account.

King does not upload your journey logs to a King-operated cloud database or other King-operated server.

3. Information King does not collect

King does not ask you to provide or collect the following information through the app:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your journey logs on a King-operated server

King does not currently use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the app.

King does not sell your personal information.

King does not provide cloud synchronization of your journey data.

If you voluntarily contact us by email, the information you provide in that communication may be received by us so that we can respond to your request.

4. Backup and restore

King allows you to export your progress to a file using your device’s file-sharing or save functionality.

You may then store that file wherever you choose, such as on your device or another storage service available through your device.

When you import a backup, King reads the file you select and uses the information in it to restore your journey data.

King does not receive your backup files unless you voluntarily send them to us or otherwise provide them to a third party.

Backup files may contain information about your journey. You are responsible for keeping exported backup files secure.

5. King Premium and Google Play

If you purchase or subscribe to King Premium, payment and subscription processing are handled by Google Play.

Google may process information required to complete and manage your purchase according to Google’s own terms and privacy policy.

King receives billing-related information or status made available through Google Play that is necessary to provide, verify, or restore Premium access.

King may store limited Premium-related information locally on your device, such as trial or access information, so that Premium features can continue to function.

This local information is not a replacement for Google’s billing records.

For more information about Google’s handling of information, please see the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Notifications and permissions

King includes an optional daily reminder feature.

If you enable reminders, the app may request permissions such as:

  • Notifications — to display your daily reminder
  • Exact alarms — on supported Android versions, to schedule a reminder at the time you select

You can decline these permissions or turn reminders off.

The core journey-tracking features do not require notification or exact-alarm permissions.

If the required permissions are unavailable, reminder scheduling may be limited or unavailable.

7. Internet access

King may use an internet connection for features that interact with Google Play, including:

  • Loading Premium product information
  • Completing Premium purchases
  • Restoring or verifying Premium access

Your normal journey logging does not require an internet connection.

King does not require an internet connection to record your ordinary daily journey activity.

8. Children

King is not directed at children under 13.

King does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Because journey data is stored locally on the device, if a child has used King, the data can be removed from the device using Reset All Data within the app or by uninstalling the app.

9. How to delete your data

You can delete your locally stored King journey data in several ways.

Within King

Use:

Settings → Reset All Data

This removes the journey data stored by the app on the device.

By uninstalling King

Uninstalling the app generally removes its locally stored app data, subject to the behavior of your device and operating system.

Backup files

If you exported a backup file and saved it somewhere outside the app, that file is separate from the app’s local data.

You must delete those files yourself from the location where you saved them.

Because King does not maintain a cloud copy of your journey data, there is no King server account or cloud profile that you need to request deletion from.

10. Data retention

King retains locally stored journey information on your device until you:

  • Reset the app’s data
  • Replace the data by importing another backup
  • Uninstall the app
  • Otherwise remove the relevant local data through your device

King does not retain copies of your journey logs on King-operated servers.

Exported backup files may remain wherever you choose to save them until you delete them.

11. Security

King stores its normal app data within the app’s private storage on your device, subject to the security protections provided by your device and Android operating system.

Exported backup files are outside the app’s private storage once you save or share them. Their security depends on where you store them and who has access to that location.

You should keep exported backup files private if they contain information you do not want others to see.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if King changes, for example if we introduce new features, cloud synchronization, analytics, or other services that affect how information is handled.

When we make changes, we will update this policy and its effective date on the published version of this page.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about King, you can contact us at:
